# 05. 원암 풀업·건 적응·단측 훈련 (`oap-tendon-unilateral`)

> 2026-10-15 REF5 근거 평가 워크플로의 원자료다. 본문은 조사·검증 에이전트가 쓴 영어 원문 그대로다. 상태: confirmed(원문 확인), corrected(출처는 있으나 서지·수치를 고침, `검증 결과`를 쓸 것), unverifiable(확인 불가, 재확인 전 근거로 쓰지 말 것), misattributed(저자 귀속 오류).

## 조사 범위

ONE-ARM PULL-UP (OAP) skill acquisition, unilateral/bilateral specificity, eccentrics, tendon adaptation and injury risk (elbow, plus the athlete's knee). Scope: REF5 v1.4 OAP slot (§7.3, §7.5.1-7.5.5, §19.3-19.6, §22.4) and the decision to keep the knee outside the program.

## 출처 (53건: confirmed 24, unverifiable 15, corrected 14)

### OT-01 · confirmed

**Kassiano W, Nunes JP, Costa B, Ribeiro AS, Loenneke JP, Cyrino ES (2025). Sports Med 55:923-936**

- meta-analysis · 2025 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-024-02169-z
- 조사 요약: 9 studies. Hypertrophy: no difference between unilateral and bilateral training (ES -0.21, 95% CI -3.56 to 3.13). Bilateral strength was favored by bilateral training (ES 0.56, 0.16-0.96). Unilateral strength was favored by unilateral training (ES -0.65, -0.93 to -0.37). Risk of bias was moderate. The authors conclude that strength gains follow specificity.
- 검증 결과: 9 studies (out of 703 screened), robust variance estimation. Hypertrophy: no difference (ES -0.21, 95% CI -3.56 to 3.13, P=0.57). Bilateral strength favored bilateral training (ES 0.56, 0.16-0.96). Unilateral strength favored unilateral training (ES -0.65, -0.93 to -0.37). Moderate risk of bias. Conclusion: strength gains appear to follow specificity.
- 검증 메모: Verified on the Springer abstract and full-text PDF. The hypertrophy estimate pools only about 2 studies (df=1), which explains the very wide CI; it is uninformative, not evidence of equivalence. The claim that most included studies were lower-limb was not checked.
- REF5 관련성: This is direct evidence that a one-arm task needs one-arm practice. Bilateral weighted pull-ups alone will under-train OAP-specific strength, which justifies having a dedicated OAP slot. Most included studies tested the lower limbs, so applying this to elbow flexors is an extrapolation.
- 조사 당시 표기: Kassiano W, Nunes JP, Costa B, Ribeiro AS, Loenneke JP, Cyrino ES (2025). Comparison of Muscle Growth and Dynamic Strength Adaptations Induced by Unilateral and Bilateral Resistance Training: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Sports Medicine 55:923-936
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39794667/

### OT-02 · confirmed

**Spitz RW, Kataoka R, Dankel SJ, et al., Loenneke JP (2023). Quantifying the Generality of Strength Adaptation: A Meta-Analysis. Sports Med 53(3):637-648**

- meta-analysis · 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01790-0
- 조사 요약: 12 studies. Strength gains in the trained movement were about twice as large as in untrained tests of the same muscles (d about 1.84 vs about 0.8).
- 검증 결과: 12 studies. Strength in the directly trained movement: d=1.84 (95% CI 1.23-2.4). Strength in a non-trained test: d=0.8 (95% CI 0.22-1.4).
- 검증 메모: The 'untrained' tests were isometric or isokinetic tests on a different device of a muscle involved in training, so they mix a change of movement with a change of contraction mode. Published online Nov 2022, in the 2023 issue.
- REF5 관련성: Gains in weighted bilateral pull-ups will only partly carry over to OAP, so the skill slot is needed. It also means OAP practice will only partly carry over to the PULL focus lift, so the trade-off works in both directions.
- 조사 당시 표기: Spitz RW, Kataoka R, Dankel SJ, Bell ZW, Song JS, Wong V, Yamada Y, Loenneke JP (2023). Quantifying the Generality of Strength Adaptation: A Meta-Analysis. Sports Medicine 53:637-648
- 조사 당시 URL: https://www.springermedicine.com/quantifying-the-generality-of-strength-adaptation-a-meta-analysi/23728068

### OT-03 · confirmed

**Saeterbakken AH, Stien N, Paulsen G, Behm DG, Andersen V, Solstad TEJ, Prieske O (2025). Task Specificity of Dynamic Resistance Training... Sports Medicine**

- meta-analysis · 2025 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12296774/
- 조사 요약: 43 studies with 1660 participants. Dynamic strength gain SMD 0.98, while transfer to untrained isometric strength was SMD 0.42.
- 검증 결과: 43 studies, 1660 participants, 72 interventions. Dynamic strength SMD 0.98 (95% CI 0.91-1.06). Transfer to non-trained isometric strength SMD 0.42 (0.35-0.49). Hypertrophy and activation changes did not predict either effect.
- 검증 메모: The transfer measured is from dynamic training to isometric tests. This bears directly on the lock-off (isometric) part of the OAP, which strengthens the relevance claim.
- REF5 관련성: This independently confirms task specificity. Holding the lock-off at the top and passing the sticking point in an OAP are specific skills that need to be practised directly.
- 조사 당시 표기: Saeterbakken AH et al. (2025). Task Specificity of Dynamic Resistance Training and Its Transferability to Non-trained Isometric Muscle Strength: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis. Sports Medicine

### OT-04 · confirmed

**Munn J, Herbert RD, Gandevia SC (2004). J Appl Physiol 96:1861-1866**

- meta-analysis · 2004 · https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/japplphysiol.00541.2003
- 조사 요약: 13 studies. The untrained limb gained +7.8% (95% CI 4.1-11.6%), about 35% of the trained-limb gain (+20.5%).
- 검증 결과: RCTs at >=50% MVC for >=2 weeks. 17 eligible, 13 pooled (309 subjects). Contralateral limb +7.8% (95% CI 4.1-11.6%), trained limb +20.5% (16.0-25.1%), so the contralateral gain was 35.1% (20.9-49.3%) of the trained-limb effect.
- REF5 관련성: Cross-education is real but partial. This supports keeping separate per-arm ladder states (the right arm promoted while the left held) and means asymmetric progress between arms is expected.
- 조사 당시 표기: Munn J, Herbert RD, Gandevia SC (2004). Contralateral effects of unilateral resistance training: a meta-analysis. Journal of Applied Physiology 96:1861-1866

### OT-05 · confirmed

**Manca A, Dragone D, Dvir Z, Deriu F (2017). Eur J Appl Physiol 117(11):2335-2354**

- meta-analysis · 2017 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-017-3720-z
- 조사 요약: 31 studies with 785 subjects. Contralateral strength rose +11.9% on average (upper limb +9.4%, lower limb +16.4%). Eccentric training gave the largest transfer (+17.7%). Risk of bias was high.
- 검증 결과: 31 RCTs, 785 subjects. Pooled cross-education +11.9% (95% CI 9.1-14.8). Upper limb +9.4%, lower limb +16.4%. Isometric 8.2%, concentric 11.3%, eccentric 17.7%, isotonic-dynamic 15.9%. High risk of bias across studies.
- 검증 메모: The eccentric subgroup is not directly compared statistically with the others. The 'extra benefit of one-arm negatives' inference has low certainty, as the researcher said.
- REF5 관련성: This supports per-arm independent states. It also suggests one-arm negatives may give a little extra benefit to the other arm, though the certainty is low.
- 조사 당시 표기: Manca A, Dragone D, Dvir Z, Deriu F (2017). Cross-education of muscular strength following unilateral resistance training: a meta-analysis. European Journal of Applied Physiology
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28936703/

### OT-06 · corrected

**Green LA, Gabriel DA (2018). Physical Therapy Reviews 23(4-5):238-249**

- meta-analysis · 2018 · https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10833196.2018.1499272
- 조사 요약: 96 studies. In young adults the untrained limb gained +18% (SMD 0.71), and transfer was about 48-77% of the trained-limb effect.
- 검증 결과: 96 studies (141 training units), deliberately inclusive rather than RCT-only. Young able-bodied adults: +18% contralateral (SMD 0.71, 95% CI 0.60-0.83, from 86 units). Average cross-body transfer is given as 48-77% in the conclusion and 52-80% in the discussion, as a range across populations, not a young-adult figure.
- 검증 메모: The 48-77% range was misattributed to young adults. The inclusive selection probably inflates estimates compared with the RCT-only Munn 2004 and Manca 2017.
- REF5 관련성: Same direction as Munn and Manca: the arms are linked but not interchangeable. This supports per-arm ladders and not mirroring promotions across arms.
- 조사 당시 표기: Green LA, Gabriel DA (2018). The effect of unilateral training on contralateral limb strength in young, older, and patient populations: a meta-analysis of cross education. Physical Therapy Reviews (Taylor & Francis)

### OT-07 · confirmed

**Roig M, et al. (2009). Br J Sports Med 43:556-568**

- meta-analysis · 2009 · https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/43/8/556
- 조사 요약: 20 RCTs. Eccentric training beat concentric training for strength mainly when performed at higher loads, and gains were highly specific to the mode and velocity of contraction.
- 검증 결과: 20 RCTs. Eccentric training beat concentric training for total and eccentric strength only when performed at higher intensity. At comparable intensity there was no difference in total strength. Strength gains after eccentric training were more specific to velocity and contraction mode.
- REF5 관련성: This supports adding heavy one-arm negatives (supramaximal relative to concentric capacity) once the ladder reaches rung 4. Specificity means negatives add to the concentric OAP but cannot replace it.
- 조사 당시 표기: Roig M, O'Brien K, Kirk G, Murray R, McKinnon P, Shadgan B, Reid WD (2009). The effects of eccentric versus concentric resistance training on muscle strength and mass in healthy adults: a systematic review with meta-analysis. British Journal of Sports Medicine 43:556-568
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18981046/

### OT-08 · confirmed

**da Silva LSL, Goncalves LdS, et al. (2025). J Strength Cond Res 39(1):115-134**

- meta-analysis · 2025 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39652733/
- 조사 요약: 26 studies with 682 participants. Overall there was no difference in hypertrophy between eccentric and concentric training (0.285, 95% CI -0.131 to 0.701), at very low GRADE certainty. An upper-limb subgroup favored eccentric training.
- 검증 결과: 26 studies, 682 subjects. No difference in hypertrophy between eccentric and concentric training (0.285, 95% CI -0.131 to 0.701, I2 84.4%, GRADE very low). Subgroups favoring eccentric training: upper limb (p=0.018, low certainty), <=8 weeks, muscle thickness, isokinetic.
- REF5 관련성: The case for negatives rests on strength and skill (Roig), not on growing muscle. This is a minor point for the OAP slot.
- 조사 당시 표기: da Silva LSL et al. (2025). Comparison Between Eccentric vs. Concentric Muscle Actions on Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research 39(1):115-134

### OT-09 · confirmed

**Martin E, Beckham GK (2017). Med Sci Sports Exerc 49(5S):1051 (abstract)**

- RCT · 2017 · https://journals.lww.com/acsm-msse/fulltext/2017/05001/effects_of_different_pull_up_training_strategies.3081.aspx
- 조사 요약: Abstract only, small sample. Traditional, weighted, and eccentric-only pull-up training improved pull-up reps similarly over 12 weeks. The eccentric-only group had the highest dropout and needed more time.
- 검증 결과: Conference abstract. Groups: traditional (n=17), +10% bodyweight (n=7), eccentric-only 6-s negatives to failure (n=6), control (n=9). Each trained 5 sets to failure, twice a week, for 12 weeks. No between-group differences. All training groups beat control (about 9.3 to 15.3 reps). The eccentric group had the highest dropout and the largest time cost.
- 검증 메모: Very small groups; underpowered to detect differences.
- REF5 관련성: Weak direct evidence that eccentric-only work is not better than concentric or weighted work for bilateral pull-ups. This supports REF5 keeping negatives as a small add-on (1x2) rather than the main tool.
- 조사 당시 표기: Martin E, Beckham GK (2017). Effects of Different Pull Up Training Strategies on Pull Up Scores. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise 49(5S):1051 (conference abstract)

### OT-10 · corrected

**Vigouroux L, Devise M (2024). Bioengineering 11(1):85**

- RCT · 2024 · https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5354/11/1/85
- 조사 요약: 30 advanced-to-elite climbers trained twice a week for 5 weeks. Maximal pull-up strength rose 2.2-5.0% in all groups. The eccentric regimen (about 95% 1RM) gave the best maximal-strength result, and isometric training gave limited transfer.
- 검증 결과: 30 advanced to high-elite climbers: ECC n=8, ISO n=7, PLYO n=6, CTRL n=9. Pull-ups on a hangboard twice a week for 5 weeks. Weighted pull-up 1RM rose in all three training groups (+2.2% ISO to +5.0% ECC) versus control (-1.5%). Post hoc tests showed every training group beat control, but no group differed significantly from another. ECC was performed at 95% 1RM; ISO was at bodyweight (about 55-75% 1RM).
- 검증 메모: 'Best' for eccentrics is numerical only, not statistically significant. ISO's weaker result is confounded by its lower load, so it does not show that isometric work transfers poorly.
- REF5 관련성: Some direct evidence, in trained climbers with bilateral pull-ups, that heavy eccentrics help maximal pulling strength. Gains are small over short blocks, so expect slow ladder progress.
- 조사 당시 표기: Vigouroux L, Devise M (2024). Pull-Up Performance Is Affected Differently by the Muscle Contraction Regimens Practiced during Training among Climbers. Bioengineering 11(1):85

### OT-11 · corrected

**Sas-Nowosielski K, Kandzia K (2022). J Hum Kinet 84 (actual title: '...One Arm Pull-Ups on Specific Upper Body Climbing Performance')**

- RCT · 2022 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9679189/
- 조사 요약: Only 2 of 24 advanced climbers could do 4-5 unassisted one-arm pull-ups; the rest needed band assistance.
- 검증 결과: Acute PAPE crossover in 24 advanced climbers. Only 14 completed the one-arm condition. Those who could do 4-5 unassisted one-arm pull-ups (n=2) did them unassisted; the rest used band assistance at 3-5RM. The authors note OAP ability among climbers ranges 'from zero to dozen or more repetitions'.
- 검증 메모: The title was misquoted. The threshold was 4-5 unassisted reps, not a single rep, and the denominator is unclear (14 in the condition, not 24). It shows few advanced climbers had 4-5-rep OAP capacity, not that a single free OAP is rare. This is not a training RCT.
- REF5 관련성: Even in an advanced pulling population, a free OAP is rare. This sets the expected time to rung 6 at months to years, not weeks.
- 조사 당시 표기: Sas-Nowosielski K, Kandzia K (2022). Acute Effects of Post-Activation Performance Enhancement of 5RM Weighted Pull-Ups and One Arm Pull-Ups on Pull-Up Performance. Journal of Human Kinetics

### OT-12 · corrected

**Bohm S, Mersmann F, Arampatzis A (2015). Sports Med Open 1:7**

- meta-analysis · 2015 · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40798-015-0009-9
- 조사 요약: 27 studies (37 interventions, Achilles and patellar tendons only). Stiffness SMD 0.70, modulus 0.69, cross-sectional area 0.24. High intensity (>70% MVC) gave 0.90 versus 0.04 at low intensity. Contraction type made no significant difference. Interventions of 12 weeks or more gave 0.91 versus 0.81 at 8-12 weeks. The authors recommend at least 12 weeks of high-intensity loading.
- 검증 결과: 27 studies, 37 interventions (Achilles and patellar tendons only; 264 participants; all >=8 weeks). Stiffness SMD 0.70, modulus 0.69, CSA 0.24. >70% MVC/RM gave 0.90 versus 0.04 at low intensity (only 5 low-intensity interventions), a significant difference. No effect of contraction type. >=12 weeks gave 0.91 versus 0.81 at 8-12 weeks, NOT significantly different (p=0.7). Authors nonetheless recommend >12 weeks of high-intensity loading.
- 검증 메모: 'Measurable tendon change takes about 12 weeks' overstates it: 8-12-week interventions also produced large stiffness changes, and studies shorter than 8 weeks were excluded. Elbow application is extrapolation.
- REF5 관련성: Applied to elbow tendons by extrapolation. Near-maximal one-arm efforts are the high-intensity loading that drives tendon adaptation, which is good. Measurable tendon change takes about 12 weeks, so the §19.5 '2 weeks on a new rung' guideline sets a minimum number of exposures but does not guarantee tendon adaptation.
- 조사 당시 표기: Bohm S, Mersmann F, Arampatzis A (2015). Human tendon adaptation in response to mechanical loading: a systematic review and meta-analysis of exercise intervention studies on healthy adults. Sports Medicine - Open 1:7

### OT-13 · corrected

**Arampatzis A, Karamanidis K, Albracht K (2007). J Exp Biol 210:2743-2753**

- RCT · 2007 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17644689/
- 조사 요약: Over 14 weeks, tendon stiffness increased only in the high-strain group (about 4.55%), not the low-strain group (about 2.85%). Tendon adaptation appears to have a strain threshold.
- 검증 결과: Within-subject design: 11 exercisers trained one leg at high tendon strain (4.55 +/- 1.38%) and the other at low strain (2.85 +/- 0.99%) at equal frequency and volume; 10 controls. After 14 weeks, stiffness, modulus and region-specific CSA increased only in the high-strain leg. This supports a strain threshold.
- 검증 메모: 4.55% and 2.85% are the applied strain magnitudes, not stiffness increases; the original wording was misleading. This is a contralateral-leg controlled trial, not a classic RCT.
- REF5 관련성: Mechanistic support (Achilles tendon, extrapolated) that low-intensity assisted reps contribute little tendon stimulus. The upper rungs carry the tendon load, so ramping slowly through rungs 3-6 matters most.
- 조사 당시 표기: Arampatzis A, Karamanidis K, Albracht K (2007). Adaptational responses of the human Achilles tendon by modulation of the applied cyclic strain magnitude. Journal of Experimental Biology 210:2743-2753 (indexed as listed)

### OT-14 · confirmed

**Magnusson SP, Langberg H, Kjaer M (2010). Nat Rev Rheumatol 6:262-268**

- review · 2010 · https://www.nature.com/articles/nrrheum.2010.43
- 조사 요약: After exercise, tendon collagen synthesis peaks around 24 h and stays elevated for about 70-80 h. Collagen degradation also rises, so there is a net collagen loss in the first ~18-36 h and net synthesis only at ~36-72 h. Without enough rest after loading, net collagen loss may result.
- 검증 결과: Collagen synthesis rises 2-3-fold, peaks about 24 h after exercise and stays elevated for up to 70-80 h. Degradation rises earlier. Net negative collagen balance for about 18-36 h (figure: 24-36 h), then net synthesis at 36-72 h. Key point: 'Without sufficient rest (24 h) after exercise, net loss of collagen might occur'. The text adds that improved training status shortens the net-loss window.
- 검증 메모: Based on peritendinous microdialysis after running (Langberg 1999). Two caveats weaken the use for a strict 48-h rule: the review's own key point names 24 h as sufficient rest, and trained people have a shorter net-loss window. Svensson 2016 notes this synthesis probably reflects the tendon periphery.
- REF5 관련성: This is the main physiological basis for the §7.3 48-hour separation between hard pulling sessions. It comes from human lower-limb microdialysis data, so applying it to the elbow is an extrapolation. It supports spacing heavy PULL and OAP by 36-72 h, and it shows why the observed ~24 h gaps are a design gap.
- 조사 당시 표기: Magnusson SP, Langberg H, Kjaer M (2010). The pathogenesis of tendinopathy: balancing the response to loading. Nature Reviews Rheumatology 6:262-268
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20308995/

### OT-15 · confirmed

**Magnusson SP, Kjaer M (2019). J Physiol 597(5):1283-1298**

- review · 2019 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6395417/
- 조사 요약: Loading increases collagen synthesis, tendon cross-sectional area and stiffness. Adaptation is slow, and tendon remodeling lags behind muscle adaptation.
- 검증 결과: Narrative review of tendon responses to loading, unloading, ageing and injury. Its summary table lists loading as increasing anabolic signalling, CSA and stiffness.
- 검증 메모: Existence and general content were verified. The specific clause that tendon remodeling lags behind muscle adaptation was not found in the accessed abstract and table, so treat it as unverified inference.
- REF5 관련성: Muscle can outpace tendon. Upper-body strength gained on the cut and PULL focus may let the athlete reach upper rungs before the elbow tendons have adapted, which supports conservative rung pacing.
- 조사 당시 표기: Magnusson SP, Kjaer M (2019). The impact of loading, unloading, ageing and injury on the human tendon. Journal of Physiology 597(5):1283-1298

### OT-16 · confirmed

**Svensson RB, Heinemeier KM, Couppe C, Kjaer M, Magnusson SP (2016). J Appl Physiol 121(6):1237-1246**

- review · 2016 · https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/japplphysiol.00328.2016
- 조사 요약: Carbon-14 bomb-pulse data show that turnover of the adult tendon core is very slow or absent; adaptation is mainly peripheral. Resistance training still increases tendon stiffness and modulus.
- 검증 결과: 'Turnover in the core of the tendon after maturity is very slow or absent', based on 14C bomb-pulse data (Heinemeier 2013, Achilles core). Loading-induced collagen synthesis probably occurs at the tendon periphery. Resistance training can increase tendon stiffness and modulus.

- 조사 당시 표기: Svensson RB, Heinemeier KM, Couppe C, Kjaer M, Magnusson SP (2016). Effect of aging and exercise on the tendon. Journal of Applied Physiology

### OT-17 · confirmed

**Baar K (2017). Sports Med 47(Suppl 1):5-11**

- review · 2017 · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-017-0719-x
- 조사 요약: In engineered ligaments (in vitro), the collagen-synthesis signal saturates within about 10 min of loading and is refractory for about 6 h. The author proposes short bouts under 10 min separated by at least 6 h.
- 검증 결과: In engineered human ligaments (in vitro), the molecular response peaks within 10 min of loading, then switches off, and takes about 6 h to become responsive again. Recommends <10 min bouts separated by >=6 h. Also reports the response was independent of load frequency and amplitude (2.5-10% stretch).
- 검증 메모: The amplitude-independence finding conflicts with the in vivo intensity dependence in Bohm 2015 and Arampatzis 2007, so this in vitro work should not be used to set the OAP dose either way.
- REF5 관련성: This is mechanistic, in-vitro evidence only. It is consistent with REF5's low-volume OAP dose (2x3 per arm) as enough connective-tissue stimulus, but it does not show that the dose is optimal.
- 조사 당시 표기: Baar K (2017). Minimizing Injury and Maximizing Return to Play: Lessons from Engineered Ligaments. Sports Medicine 47(Suppl 1):5-11
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28332110/

### OT-19 · corrected

**Quarmby A, Zhang M, Geisler M, Javorsky T, Mugele H, Cassel M, Lawley J (2023). Front Sports Act Living 5:1269870**

- review · 2023 · https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2023.1269870/full
- 조사 요약: 34 studies. Overuse incidence was about 4.2 per 1000 h and 1-year prevalence about 50%. Medial epicondylopathy risk rose with years of experience in cross-sectional data. Prevention evidence was sparse and low quality: in one trial, elbow injury incidence fell from 1.8 to 0.5 per 1000 exposures with prevention training.
- 검증 결과: Systematic review of 34 studies (64.7% cross-sectional; quality 8-20/32), with no meta-analysis. The figures of about 4.2 injuries per 1000 h (Backe) and about 50% one-year prevalence (Jones) are cited in the introduction and cover ALL injuries, traumatic plus overuse; they are not pooled overuse results. Medial epicondylitis rising with experience comes from a single cross-sectional study, and overall evidence on age or experience was 'conflicting'. Two reports from one Kozin RCT cohort (SQS 18/32) show elbow injuries falling from 1.8 to 0.5 per 1000 exposures with a year-long programme 3-4 times a week. The program record shows OAP twice landing about 24 h after a PULL focus session: on consecutive days the focus alternates, and the density valve only forces a micro session when both of the previous two calendar days were program days. The evidence supports the 48 h intent. Direct performance data (Miranda 2018) show that 24 h of recovery impairs upper-body rep performance versus 48 or 72 h. Tendon physiology (Magnusson 2010) shows net collagen loss in the first ~18-36 h and net synthesis only at ~36-72 h; this is extrapolated from lower-limb tendons to the elbow. Coaching (Lopez) recommends 24-72 h between lock-off sessions. Under the user's own principle that a low success rate is first read as insufficient recovery, the 9/07 and 9/12 FAIL exposures should be checked against the gap since the last PULL focus session. If they coincide with ~24 h gaps, recovery explains them better than inadequate rung choice. This is the single highest-priority protocol change, proposed as a §26-style proposal for one element: the engine offers the OAP slot only when at least 48 h have passed since the start of the last PULL focus session; otherwise that BP-focus day falls back to the existing revert path (PULL volume 2x6, as in v1.3). Squat-first is fixed by the user. Moving OAP before the BP focus would be a single possible change, but it would cost the session's primary lift and has no direct evidence of net benefit, so it is low priority and not recommended ahead of the 48 h gate.

The volume dose-response curve (Pelland 2025) has strong diminishing returns, so losing about 2 moderate bilateral sets a week probably costs little weighted-pull-up strength, and the OAP pairs add indirect elbow-flexor and lat work. The weak point is in the coaching literature: readiness benchmarks (JASC 3RM at +60% bodyweight; Antranik 3x8 at +50%; DeMarco about +2/3; Lattice 165% bodyweight total) put the athlete (last PULL total about 147% bodyweight, i.e. about +47% added) at or below the threshold. For the coming months, PULL focus kg progression is probably the main driver of OAP readiness, and the ladder is secondary. The trade stays sound only while PULL focus keeps progressing. The existing judgment structure (FAIL 0 / HOLD<=1 / volume veto) is the right guardrail; no change is proposed.
- [§7.5.2 ordinal hand-position ladder, rungs 1-6, default start rung 2] Evidence is SILENT; the method is coaching-supported. No study validates self-assisted hand-on-arm ladders. The method appears in coaching sources (Hybrid Calisthenics, La Fabrique Verticale, leverage.fit). The main threat to validity is that assist force within a rung is not fixed: the assisting hand can grip and pull harder, so a PASS can be 'bought' with extra help (leverage.fit: the assistance is hard to measure). Brookshire found grips past the elbow awkward, which suggests rungs 4-5 may be uneven steps. Rung 5 being 'stability only' implies a large jump to rung 6; §22.4 already admits the rungs are not equal intervals. The 9/07 start at rung 2, followed by demotion of both arms to rung 1, suggests the default start was too high for this athlete, consistent with the §19.5 rule that the first two exposures are calibration. A possible single change concerns measurement, not judgment criteria: standardize the assisting hand's contact (for example open palm or fingers only, no closed grip) if the spec does not already define it, so that PASS/FAIL stays comparable across exposures. This does not relax any criterion.
- [§7.5.3 dose: 2x3 per arm, about 1.5 exposures per week] Evidence is SILENT on the optimum; REF5 sits at the conservative end. Coaching dose guides (Low's Prilepin-style 25-50 total reps for strength; about 2 sessions per week per leverage.fit and Lopez's 1-2 per week) place REF5's 6 reps per arm per exposure, about 9 per week, at the low-volume, elbow-conservative end. The frequency meta-analysis (Grgic 2018) shows no clear frequency effect once volume is equated; Pelland 2025 finds a small independent frequency benefit for strength. Arithmetic: reaching rung 6 from rung 1 takes at least 5 promotions x 3 PASS = 15 perfect exposures, about 10 weeks. This is an unrealistic floor; coaching anecdotes (DeMarco) and population data (Sas-Nowosielski: 2 of 24 advanced climbers could do free OAPs) suggest months to years. After 5 exposures, the left arm is still at rung 1 and the right arm has just been promoted to rung 2. With 5 data points, insufficient stimulus and insufficient recovery cannot be told apart, so no change now. If the ladder stalls after the 48 h gate is in place, the next single candidate change would be a stimulus increase (for example a third pair-set per arm). The two changes should never be made together.
- [§7.5.4 promotion and demotion: 3 consecutive PASS promotes, 2 consecutive FAIL demotes, HOLD breaks both streaks, INVALID is neutral] Evidence is SILENT; the rule fits coaching logic and should be kept. No research exists on auto-regulated skill-ladder rules. The asymmetric hysteresis (3 to promote, 2 to demote) prevents oscillation between rungs and matches how coaches progress when every set is clean. The rule has already worked as designed (demotion on 9/12, then recovery and a right-arm promotion by 10/04). At about 1.5 exposures per week, 3 PASS already takes about 2 weeks, so the §19.5 guideline of at least 2 weeks on a new rung is largely redundant with this rule. Per the user's principle, the judgment criteria are not to be relaxed, and nothing in the evidence argues for it.
- [§7.5.3 negatives from rung 4: 1x2 per arm, 5-8 s controlled descent] SUPPORTED, both in dose and gating. The eccentric meta-analysis (Roig 2009) favors eccentric training for strength when loads are high, with mode-specific gains, which fits supramaximal one-arm negatives. Vigouroux 2024, in elite climbers, found the heavy eccentric regimen best for maximal pull-up strength. Martin & Beckham 2017 (abstract only) found eccentric-only pull-up training no better than standard training and with more dropouts, which supports keeping negatives as an add-on. Coaching (Low 3-5 s progressing to 7-10 s; Hooper 5 s) matches the 5-8 s tempo; Low uses more volume (2-3 sets of 2-3 reps), so REF5's 1x2 is conservative. Unlocking only at rung 4 matches Antranik's warning that rushed negatives cause elbow pain. The risk is the end of the rep: dropping into a supinated dead hang. Rupture-mechanism video data (Jukes 2022: supinated 98%, near-straight elbow 89%) are extrapolated from mostly deadlift cases. An operational note, not a criteria change: finish each negative in a controlled stop just short of full lockout and step down, rather than dropping into the hang.
- [§19.5 guideline: stay at least 2 weeks on a new rung; conservative tendon framing] The tendon evidence is EXTRAPOLATED and supports caution, but 2 weeks is not tendon-adaptive. The tendon meta-analysis (Bohm 2015, Achilles and patellar tendons only) shows adaptation needs high intensity (>70% MVC: SMD 0.90 vs 0.04 at low intensity) and roughly 12 weeks or more. Muscle can outpace tendon (Magnusson & Kjaer 2019). The 2-week minimum is therefore a pacing floor, not a guarantee of tendon readiness. The upper rungs (4-6), where intensity crosses the strain threshold (Arampatzis 2007), are where pacing matters most. The current rules (3 PASS plus at least 2 weeks plus the SAFETY close) are reasonable; no change is proposed. Evidence specific to elbow tendons does not exist.
- [§19.4/§19.5 elbow monitoring: SAFETY close on discomfort, consider revert if pain occurs on 2 consecutive exposures, no climbing on the day of or before OAP, fixed grip] SUPPORTED by epidemiology and coaching, with one gap. Elbows account for about 17.7% of chronic climbing injuries (Gronhaug 2018). Medial epicondylopathy and brachialis 'climber's elbow' are the main entities (CSMR 2023). Overuse prevalence is about 50% per year (Quarmby 2023, mostly cross-sectional), and the athlete's climbing adds to the elbow load. Grip choice changes which tissue is loaded (Urbanczyk 2020 modeling; Nelson coaching), which supports keeping the grip fixed. The gap: REF5 monitors pain during the session, but the tendon-loading literature (Malliaras 2015) and coaching (Lopez: stop if there is elbow pain the next day) weight the 24-h response more heavily. A logging suggestion, outside the protocol and not a judgment criterion: record next-morning elbow pain (0-10) after OAP and climbing days, and treat a rising trend as the trigger for the existing revert path. The low-quality prevention RCT (Kozin 2022) only weakly supports the existing warm-up wrist-flexor eccentric; it does not justify adding accessories.
- [Data hygiene: bodyweight logged as 75 every session during a cut toward 70] Evidence SUPPORTS continuing the cut; the logging undermines the PULL data. Energy deficit does not blunt strength gains (Murphy & Koehler 2022: ES 0.84 vs 0.81), so the 0.25%/week cut is compatible with OAP and PULL progress. Falling from 75 to 70 kg lowers the one-arm load by about 6.7%, so part of future ladder progress will come from body-mass loss, not strength. Logging a default 75 kg also misstates PULL total load (bodyweight plus added load), which §22.4 already flags as bodyweight-dependent. Operational suggestion, not a protocol change: weigh at least weekly and log the actual bodyweight.
- [Per-arm independent OAP state] SUPPORTED. Cross-education meta-analyses (Munn 2004: about 35% transfer; Manca 2017: +11.9%; Green & Gabriel 2018: 48-77% in young adults) show the arms are linked but not interchangeable. Separate per-arm ladders (R promoted, L in HOLD) and asymmetric progress between arms are expected and correct. The evidence does not justify mirroring promotions across arms.
- [Knee kept outside the program: suprapatellar pain, undiagnosed, managed with depth and posture control plus warm-up isometric leg extensions 45 s x4-5 at pain 0-3/10] DEFENSIBLE, with caveats. (1) Diagnosis: the symptom profile (stairs, rising from a chair) fits the consensus definition of patellofemoral pain (Crossley 2016) as well as quadriceps tendinopathy. Quadriceps-tendon evidence is sparse and extrapolated from patellar tendon data (King 2019). A clinical diagnosis is recommended (Malliaras 2015), because the better depth strategy differs by diagnosis (Hartmann 2013: patellofemoral stress peaks around 90 degrees, while deeper flexion adds tendon-femur compression; Cook & Purdam 2012 compression hypothesis). (2) The warm-up isometric is reasonable but not therapeutic in itself. Rio 2015 (n=6) reported dramatic pain relief, but van Ark 2016 (RCT n=29), Holden 2020 (pre-registered, n=21; 0.9 NRS, not sustained) and Pearson 2020 found isometrics no better than isotonic loading. (3) The evidence-based long-term treatment is progressive heavy or moderate slow loading (Kongsgaard 2009 HSR; Agergaard 2021, where heavy and moderate loads were equal; Breda 2021, progressive loading with pain at or below 3/10). REF5's heavy squats already are this kind of loading, so the program is the knee's main loading stimulus even though it is formally out of scope. (4) The missing element is a 24-h response record: next-morning pain on a fixed test (a stair or single-leg decline squat, NRS) tracked week to week. This is operational and outside the protocol, consistent with keeping the knee out of the program's rules. (5) Depth control is a plausible way to modify symptoms, but partial range of motion costs strength (Pallares 2021: lower-limb 1RM ES 1.53 favoring full ROM), and in healthy trained men the half-squat group was the only one with increased pain (Pallares 2020). Depth limits should be temporary, with a return to full depth when the 24-h response allows. (6) As the user already holds, the automatic micro session should not be read as knee rehab; the evidence supports that view.
- [Overall horizon for the OAP slot (§22.4 known weaknesses)] Evidence is SILENT on REF5 specifically but informs expectations. Free OAPs are rare even among advanced climbers (Sas-Nowosielski 2022: 2 of 24). Coaching timeframes run from months to about 3 years (DeMarco; JASC practitioner model). The value of OAP rests on the athlete's goal, not on performance necessity (Low 2020). §22.4 could state an expected horizon of many months, so that a slow ladder is not misread as protocol failure. This is a documentation change, not a protocol change.

## 불확실성

- No RCT or cohort study exists on one-arm pull-up or chin-up skill acquisition, or on self-assisted hand-on-arm ladders. All ladder-design judgments rest on coaching sources and extrapolation from bilateral pull-up and general strength studies.
- The readiness benchmarks (+50 to +67% bodyweight weighted pull-ups; 165% bodyweight total) are practitioner heuristics, not validated thresholds; the JASC model's authors and details were not verified here.
- The tendon adaptation meta-analysis (Bohm 2015) and the collagen time-course data (Magnusson 2010) come from lower-limb tendons. The time course and safe loading rate for elbow-flexor and common-flexor tendons are unknown, and the Baar 6-h refractory data are from in-vitro engineered ligaments.
- The 48 h contradiction is based on the program's DB record (two ~24 h gaps) and on reading the engine's density-valve and focus-alternation logic. The engine source (packages/core/src/program-engine/ref5.ts) was not read here, and whether the 9/07 and 9/12 FAIL exposures coincided with those gaps was not checked.
- Only 5 OAP exposures exist. Insufficient stimulus and insufficient recovery as explanations for the left arm's slow progress cannot be distinguished yet.
- Logged bodyweight (75 every session) is probably a default and not measured, so relative-strength figures (about 147% bodyweight total, about +47% added) carry an error of a few percent.
- Rupture-mechanism data (Jukes 2022) mostly involve deadlifts. Extending them to the bottom position of a one-arm chin-up is biomechanical reasoning, not observed injury data.
- Climbing injury data are mostly cross-sectional self-report. Prevention trials (Kozin 2022) are small and low quality, and how much risk the OAP adds on top of climbing cannot be quantified.
- Evidence on isometric pain relief for tendinopathy conflicts, with small samples and short follow-up. Specific evidence for quadriceps tendinopathy is sparse and extrapolated from patellar tendinopathy.
- The knee is undiagnosed (patellofemoral pain vs quadriceps tendinopathy vs other). Depth recommendations differ by diagnosis, so any knee implication is provisional.
- The specificity and cross-education meta-analyses mostly involve lower-limb or single-joint tasks, with moderate-to-high risk of bias. Transfer sizes for elbow flexors in already-trained pullers are uncertain.
- The cost to weighted pull-up strength of losing about 2 weekly PULL volume sets is inferred from general dose-response meta-regression (Pelland 2025), not measured for this lift or population.
- The Vigouroux paper's publication year (journal volume 11, issue 1, i.e. 2024) and some details of the cited conference abstracts (Martin & Beckham 2017) are taken from abstracts only; full texts were not reviewed.
