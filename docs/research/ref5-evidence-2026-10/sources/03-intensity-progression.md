# 03. 강도·진행 모델 (`intensity-progression`)

> 2026-10-15 REF5 근거 평가 워크플로의 원자료다. 본문은 조사·검증 에이전트가 쓴 영어 원문 그대로다. 상태: confirmed(원문 확인), corrected(출처는 있으나 서지·수치를 고침, `검증 결과`를 쓸 것), unverifiable(확인 불가, 재확인 전 근거로 쓰지 말 것), misattributed(저자 귀속 오류).

## 조사 범위

LOAD/INTENSITY and PROGRESSION MODELS for maximal strength: heavy vs moderate loads, specificity/practice effect, periodization models, increment and stall/reset rules, deloads/taper, detraining, and realistic rates of strength gain, applied to REF5 v1.4

## 출처 (44건: confirmed 26, unverifiable 10, corrected 8)

### IP-01 · confirmed

**Currier BS, Mcleod JC, Banfield L, et al. (2023). Resistance training prescription for muscle strength and hypertrophy in healthy adults: a systematic review and Bayesian network meta-analysis. Br J Sports Med 57(18):1211-1220**

- meta-analysis · 2023 · https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/57/18/1211
- 조사 요약: Strength network: 178 RCTs, n=5097. All prescriptions beat control. Higher-load (>80% 1RM) prescriptions maximised strength. Top-ranked: higher-load, multi-set, 3x/week (SMD 1.60, 95% CrI 1.38-1.82 vs control). Hypertrophy was similar across loads. The authors called the ranking robust in threshold analysis.
- 검증 결과: Strength network: 178 studies, n=5097 (45% women). Higher-load (>80% 1RM) prescriptions maximised strength. Top-ranked was higher-load, multi-set, 3x/week (HM3): SMD 1.60 (95% CrI 1.38-1.82) vs control. All prescriptions produced comparable hypertrophy; the top hypertrophy prescription was HM2 (SMD 0.66). The abstract calls the results 'extremely robust' in threshold analysis.
- 검증 메모: Read from the BMJ page because PMC was behind a CAPTCHA. The main text qualifies the robustness claim: HM3's top rank was sensitive in 65 comparisons, and in 92% of those HM2 became top-ranked. So 'high load, multi-set' is robust, but the 3x vs 2x ordering is not.
- REF5 관련성: Benchmark for the SQ hard zone (H3 ~80-82%, H2 ~85-87% of the stated e1RM) and for multi-set, multi-day exposure. V (~70%) and DL 2x4 (~72%) fall below the top-ranked load band.
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10579494/

### IP-02 · confirmed

**Lopez P, Radaelli R, Taaffe DR, et al. (2021). Resistance Training Load Effects on Muscle Hypertrophy and Strength Gain: Systematic Review and Network Meta-analysis. Med Sci Sports Exerc 53(6):1206-1216**

- meta-analysis · 2021 · https://jyx.jyu.fi/jyx/Record/jyx_123456789_75929
- 조사 요약: 28 studies, 747 adults, all sets to volitional failure. Strength: high load (<=8RM) and moderate load (9-15RM) both beat low load (>15RM) (SMD 0.60-0.63 and 0.34-0.35). High load beat moderate load non-significantly (SMD 0.26-0.28, P=.068). Hypertrophy did not depend on load.
- 검증 결과: 28 studies, 747 healthy adults, all sets to volitional failure. Strength: high load (<=8RM) vs low (>15RM) SMD 0.60-0.63; moderate (9-15RM) vs low SMD 0.34-0.35; high vs moderate SMD 0.26-0.28, P=.068 (not significant). Hypertrophy did not differ by load.
- REF5 관련성: Supports keeping the SQ, BP and PULL focus work in a <=8RM-equivalent zone. The high-vs-moderate edge is small and imprecise, and these studies trained to failure, which REF5 does not.

### IP-04 · confirmed

**Schoenfeld BJ, Grgic J, Ogborn D, Krieger JW (2017). Strength and hypertrophy adaptations between low- vs. high-load resistance training: a systematic review and meta-analysis. J Strength Cond Res 31(12):3508-3523**

- meta-analysis · 2017 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28834797/
- 조사 요약: 21 studies, sets to failure, low load <=60% vs high load >60% 1RM. 1RM gains were significantly greater with high loads. Isometric strength and hypertrophy were similar. Effect-size numbers were not visible on the page fetched.
- 검증 결과: 21 studies. All sets went to momentary failure, comparing low load (<=60% 1RM) with high load (>60% 1RM). 1RM gains were significantly greater with high loads. Isometric strength and hypertrophy did not differ. The abstract reports no effect sizes.
- REF5 관련성: Foundational support for load magnitude driving 1RM, as distinct from hypertrophy.

### IP-05 · corrected

**Schoenfeld BJ, Grgic J, Van Every DW, Plotkin DL (2021). Loading Recommendations for Muscle Strength, Hypertrophy, and Local Endurance: A Re-Examination of the Repetition Continuum. Sports 9(2):32**

- review · 2021 · https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4663/9/2/32
- 조사 요약: Proposes a revised paradigm: hypertrophy can be achieved across a wide range of loads, but maximal strength is best served by heavy loads (1-5 reps, 80-100% 1RM) because of specificity.
- 검증 결과: The review re-examines the traditional repetition continuum, which assigns 1-5 reps at 80-100% to strength. It proposes that adaptations can be obtained across a wide range of loads. For strength, the evidence supports a heavy-load advantage when strength is tested as 1RM on the trained exercise. That advantage largely disappears on isometric tests. Light loads still build strength, just less.
- 검증 메모: The 1-5 reps at 80-100% range is the traditional model the authors examine, not their own revised recommendation. Their point is that the heavy-load edge is test-specific: it shows up on 1RM, not isometrics. The REF5 use (heavy work is strength-specific) still holds.
- REF5 관련성: Frames REF5's 3x3/3x2 hard work as strength-specific. V 3x5 at ~70% sits at the low edge of the classic strength zone.
- 조사 당시 URL: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7927075/

Training to failure, complex periodization and machines vs free weights did not consistently affect outcomes for average healthy adults. Main message: adherence and consistency.

### IP-07 · unverifiable

**American College of Sports Medicine (2009). Position stand: Progression models in resistance training for healthy adults. Med Sci Sports Exerc 41(3):687-708**

- position stand · 2009 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19204579/
- 조사 요약: Intermediate and advanced lifters: periodized 1-12RM work with eventual emphasis on 1-6RM and 3-5 min rest. Increase load 2-10% when the lifter exceeds target reps by 1-2 on two consecutive sessions (evidence category B). Strength loading for experienced lifters: 80-100% 1RM.
- 검증 결과: Not fetched: PubMed returned a reCAPTCHA page and the journals.lww.com full text failed to load. From my background knowledge (not verified this session), the abstract recommends: novice-intermediate strength at 60-70% 1RM for 8-12 reps; advanced lifters cycling 80-100% 1RM; intermediate-advanced work across 1-12RM with eventual emphasis on 1-6RM and 3-5 min rest; and a 2-10% load increase when the lifter exceeds target reps by 1-2 on two consecutive sessions.
- 검증 메모: This is a canonical, real document. Two specific details could not be confirmed this session: the 'evidence category B' label for the progression rule, and the claim in implication 11 that small-muscle lifts should get smaller steps.
- REF5 관련성: REF5's +2.5 kg step is 2.8-3.4% for SQ, BP, PULL and DL, at the low end of the 2-10% guidance. OHP's step is 7.7%. REF5's 4-6 exposure windows are more conservative than the classic two-session rule. REF5 rests 150-240 s, so its shorter 150 s rests fall below the 3-5 min guidance.

### IP-09 · unverifiable

**Mangine GT, Hoffman JR, Gonzalez AM, et al. (2015). The effect of training volume and intensity on improvements in muscular strength and size in resistance-trained men. Physiol Rep 3(8):e12472**

- RCT · 2015 · https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4562558/
- 조사 요약: Trained men, 8 weeks, 4 d/wk. 4x3-5 at ~90% 1RM (3-min rest) vs 4x10-12 at ~70% (1-min rest). Bench 1RM gain was greater with high intensity (+14.8% vs +6.9%). Squat 1RM improved in both groups with no difference. Load was raised after all reps were completed on two consecutive workouts.
- 검증 결과: Not verified. PMC returned a CAPTCHA and the physiology.org and Wiley mirrors failed to load. The bench +14.8% vs +6.9% result, the squat no-difference result, and the 'two consecutive workouts' progression rule could not be checked.
- 검증 메모: Treat the numbers as unconfirmed. The study is likely real, but it is a single small RCT in which rest interval was confounded with load.
- REF5 관련성: Direct trained-lifter evidence that heavier, lower-rep work favours upper-body 1RM. Supports BP and PULL focus at 3x3. The squat result suggests moderate loads can also progress the squat.

### IP-10 · corrected

**Androulakis-Korakakis P, Fisher JP, Steele J (2020). The Minimum Effective Training Dose Required to Increase 1RM Strength in Resistance-Trained Men: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Sports Med 50(4):751-765**

- meta-analysis · 2020 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31797219/
- 조사 요약: 6 studies. A single set of 6-12 reps at ~70-85% 1RM, 1-3x/week to failure, for 8-12 weeks gave 'suboptimal yet significant' gains: SQ +17.48 kg (95% CI 8.51-26.46), BP +8.25 kg (0.68-15.83).
- 검증 결과: 6 studies met inclusion (5 meta-analysed). Conclusion: a single set of 6-12 reps at ~70-85% 1RM, 2-3 sessions/week, to failure, for 8-12 weeks gives suboptimal but significant gains. Squat +17.48 kg (95% CI 8.51-26.46), bench +8.25 kg (0.68-15.83).
- 검증 메모: Frequency is 2-3x/week per the abstract, not 1-3x. The author list was not shown on the fetched page.
- REF5 관련성: Shows low-dose work still moves 1RM in trained men. REF5's 2x/week minimum is above this floor, but these minimal doses were taken to failure.

### IP-11 · corrected

**Androulakis-Korakakis P, Michalopoulos N, Fisher JP, Keogh J, Gentil P, Steele J (2021). The Minimum Effective Training Dose Required for 1RM Strength in Powerlifters. Front Sports Act Living 3:713655**

- other · 2021 · https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/sports-and-active-living/articles/10.3389/fspor.2021.713655/full
- 조사 요약: Mixed-methods series (interviews, surveys, two training studies in intermediate-advanced powerlifters). A minimal dose is ~3-6 weekly sets of 1-5 reps per lift at >80% 1RM and RPE 7.5-9.5, spread over 1-3 sessions. Single-only training gives less meaningful gains. Adding 2-3 back-off sets at ~80% of the single may help over 6 weeks.
- 검증 결과: Original research with five studies: two interview studies, a survey, and two 6-week training interventions (n=25 analysed). Proposed minimal dose for raw powerlifters: ~3-6 working sets/week of 1-5 reps at >80% 1RM, RPE ~7.5-9.5, across 1-3 sessions per lift, over 6-12 weeks. Single-only training at RPE 9-9.5 is time-efficient but gains are 'less likely to be meaningful'. Adding 2-3 back-off sets at ~80% of the single 'may' produce greater gains; the interventions used 2x3 at 80% of the daily max.
- 검증 메모: Author list is wrong. The correct list is Androulakis-Korakakis, Michalopoulos, Fisher, Keogh, Loenneke, Helms, Wolf, Nuckols, Steele. Gentil is not an author.
- REF5 관련성: The closest analogue to REF5's per-lift dose: SQ hard 6 sets/week at >80%, BP focus 3x3. REF5 sits at or above this minimum. REF5 omits the heavy-single component by design (no 1RM tests).

### IP-12 · confirmed

**Mattocks KT, Buckner SL, Jessee MB, Dankel SJ, Mouser JG, Loenneke JP (2017). Practicing the Test Produces Strength Equivalent to Higher Volume Training. Med Sci Sports Exerc 49(9):1945-1954**

- RCT · 2017 · https://www.researchwithrowan.com/en/publications/practicing-the-test-produces-strength-equivalent-to-higher-volume/
- 조사 요약: 38 untrained subjects, 8 weeks. Just practicing the 1RM (up to 5 single attempts per visit) gave 1RM gains equal to 4 sets of 8-12RM to failure. Differences: upper body -1.1 kg (90% CI -4.8 to 2.4), lower body +1.0 kg (-0.7 to 2.8). Hypertrophy was greater in the high-volume group.
- 검증 결과: 38 untrained subjects, 8 weeks. Knee extension and chest press only. TEST group did up to 5 single 1RM attempts per visit; HYPER group did 4 sets to failure (~8-12RM). 1RM differences: upper -1.1 kg (90% CI -4.8 to 2.4); lower (dominant leg) 1.0 kg (-0.7 to 2.8). Neither differed from zero. Muscle size increased more with HYPER at most sites.
- 검증 메모: The exercises were machine and single-joint lifts (knee extension, chest press) in untrained people. Transfer to a trained barbell squatter is an extrapolation.
- REF5 관련성: 'Strength is a skill': 1RM expression responds to near-max practice. REF5 never trains above ~87-90%, so 1RM expression skill goes untrained. This is a cost of C9 when G1 is framed as a 1RM, though not a problem for training progress itself. The subjects were untrained.

### IP-13 · unverifiable

**Saeterbakken AH, Stien N, Paulsen G, Behm DG, Andersen V, Solstad TEJ, et al. (2025). Task Specificity of Dynamic Resistance Training and Its Transferability to Non-trained Isometric Muscle Strength: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis. Sports Med**

- meta-analysis · 2025 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40314751/
- 조사 요약: 43 studies, 1660 participants. Dynamic strength gain SMD 0.98 (0.91-1.06) vs transfer to untrained isometric tests SMD 0.42 (0.35-0.49), a 2.2-2.6x specificity ratio. Trained lifters showed the smallest effects (dynamic SMD 0.75). Hypertrophy did not predict strength change.
- 검증 결과: Could not access the PubMed record (CAPTCHA on repeated attempts) and found no alternative host. I could not confirm the existence, author list, 43 studies/1660 participants, or the SMDs (0.98 dynamic vs 0.42 isometric; 0.75 in trained).
- 검증 메모: Do not lean on the '2.2-2.6x specificity ratio' until it is verified. The qualitative point that dynamic 1RM gains exceed isometric transfer is independently supported by Schoenfeld 2017 and 2021, both confirmed.
- REF5 관련성: Recent support for strong task specificity. Progress shows up best in the trained lift and loading pattern (high-bar 3x3/3x2). The squat KPI should be read from the trained task, not inferred from other tasks.

### IP-14 · confirmed

**Nuzzo JL, Pinto MD, Nosaka K, Steele J (2024; online Oct 2023). Maximal Number of Repetitions at Percentages of the One Repetition Maximum. Sports Med 54:303-321**

- meta-analysis · 2024 · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-023-01937-7
- 조사 요약: 952 reps-to-failure tests, 7289 people. Main-model means were ~5 reps at 90% and ~15 at 70% 1RM. Bench: ~4 reps at 90%, ~9 (8.8) at 80%, ~14 at 70%. Between-person SD ~2.5 reps at 80% (4.4 at 60%). Training status and sex had little effect.
- 검증 결과: 952 reps-to-failure tests, 7289 individuals (452 groups, 269 studies), 60% resistance-trained. Both the mean and the SD of reps decrease as %1RM rises. Leg press allows more reps than bench press across loads. Sex, age and training status had little influence. The authors chose a natural cubic spline because a log-linear model overestimated reps at high %1RM (~5 reps at 95%).
- 검증 메모: Existence, sample and moderator claims are confirmed. The specific numbers (~5 reps at 90%, ~8.8 bench at 80%, ~15 at 70%, SD ~2.5 at 80%) are in tables I could not access, so they are unverified. Volume is 54(2), Feb 2024. Author list includes others before Steele.
- REF5 관련성: Lets us estimate REF5 proximity: H3 3x3 at ~82% leaves ~4-6 reps in reserve, H2 3x2 at ~87% ~3-5, V 3x5 at ~70% ~8-10. The large individual SD means %1RM-to-REF mapping is uncertain by roughly +/-5%.
- 조사 당시 표기: Nuzzo JL, Pinto MD, Nosaka K, Steele J (2024; online Oct 2023). Maximal Number of Repetitions at Percentages of the One Repetition Maximum: A Meta-Regression and Moderator Analysis of Sex, Age, Training Status, and Exercise. Sports Med 54:303-321
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10933212/

### IP-16 · confirmed

**Pelland JC, Remmert JF, Robinson ZP, Hinson SR, Zourdos MC (2024 preprint). The Resistance Training Dose-Response: Meta-Regressions Exploring the Effects of Weekly Volume and Frequency on Muscle Hypertrophy and Strength Gain. SportRxiv**

- meta-analysis · 2024 · https://sportrxiv.org/index.php/server/preprint/view/460
- 조사 요약: 67 studies, 2058 participants. Strength rises with weekly volume but with much stronger diminishing returns than hypertrophy. Posterior probability of a positive frequency effect on strength was 100%, also with diminishing returns. Frequency effects on hypertrophy were negligible. Publication status not verified.
- 검증 결과: Preprint v2, 4 Oct 2024. 67 studies, 2058 participants. Volume: posterior probability of a positive effect was 100% for both hypertrophy and strength, with diminishing returns much more pronounced for strength. Frequency: posterior probability of a positive effect on strength was 100%, also with diminishing returns. Frequency effects on hypertrophy were compatible with negligible.
- 검증 메모: The page shows no journal version, so treat it as a preprint.
- REF5 관련성: Supports spreading SQ exposures (~2.9/week including V at ~2.9 sessions/week) rather than concentrating them. Moderate set counts are enough for strength because returns diminish.

### IP-17 · confirmed

**Grgic J, Schoenfeld BJ, Davies TB, Lazinica B, Krieger JW, Pedisic Z (2018). Effect of Resistance Training Frequency on Gains in Muscular Strength: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis. Sports Med 48(5):1207-1220**

- meta-analysis · 2018 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29470825/
- 조사 요약: 22 studies. Strength ES rose with frequency: 0.74 (1x), 0.82 (2x), 0.93 (3x), 1.08 (4+x/week). Volume-equated studies showed no frequency effect (p=0.421). The frequency effect was significant for multi-joint 1RM. Most participants were untrained.
- 검증 결과: 22 studies. ES by weekly frequency: 0.74 (1x), 0.82 (2x), 0.93 (3x), 1.08 (4+x). Volume-equated: no significant frequency effect (p=0.421). The frequency effect was significant for multi-joint tests (p<0.001), not single-joint (p=0.324). Most participants were untrained.
- REF5 관련성: At 2 sessions/week, REF5 loses mainly volume and exposures per calendar time, so windows lengthen. Per-exposure effectiveness is not necessarily lower.

### IP-18 · confirmed

**Moesgaard L, Beck MM, Christiansen L, Aagaard P, Lundbye-Jensen J (2022). Effects of Periodization on Strength and Muscle Hypertrophy in Volume-Equated Resistance Training Programs: A Systematic Review and Meta-analysis. Sports Med 52:1647-1666**

- meta-analysis · 2022 · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-021-01636-1
- 조사 요약: 35 studies. Periodized beat non-periodized for 1RM (ES 0.31, 95% CI 0.04-0.57). Undulating beat linear (ES 0.31, 0.02-0.61), but only in trained participants (ES 0.61, 0.00-1.22). Untrained showed no difference (0.06). Hypertrophy was unaffected.
- 검증 결과: 35 studies. 1RM: periodized vs non-periodized ES 0.31 (0.04-0.57); undulating vs linear ES 0.31 (0.02-0.61). Undulating advantage in trained ES 0.61 (0.00-1.22), P=0.05; untrained ES 0.06 (-0.20 to 0.31). Hypertrophy was not affected.
- REF5 관련성: REF5's H3/H2/V rotation is a form of daily undulation, which this meta-analysis weakly supports for trained lifters. The CI touches zero.
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35044672/

### IP-19 · unverifiable
