# 02. 실패 근접도·자동조절 (`proximity-autoregulation`)

> 2026-10-15 REF5 근거 평가 워크플로의 원자료다. 본문은 조사·검증 에이전트가 쓴 영어 원문 그대로다. 상태: confirmed(원문 확인), corrected(출처는 있으나 서지·수치를 고침, `검증 결과`를 쓸 것), unverifiable(확인 불가, 재확인 전 근거로 쓰지 말 것), misattributed(저자 귀속 오류).

## 조사 범위

Proximity to failure, set termination ("stop at the first obvious slowdown", PASS/HOLD/FAIL) and autoregulation in REF5 v1.4

## 출처 (47건: confirmed 42, corrected 5)

### PA-01 · confirmed

**Robinson ZP, Pelland JC, Remmert JF, Refalo MC, Jukic I, Steele J, Zourdos MC. Sports Med. 2024;54(9):2209-2231. doi:10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2**

- meta-analysis · 2024 · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2
- 조사 요약: Exploratory multilevel meta-regressions with RIR estimated after the fact for each study. Strength model: 243 effects from 55 studies. The RIR slope for strength had CIs containing zero, so strength gains were similar across a wide RIR range. Hypertrophy model: 140 effects from 26 studies. The RIR slope was negative and excluded zero (beta about -0.019 to -0.023 SMC per RIR), so hypertrophy rises as sets end closer to failure. All models adjusted for load, and the authors' key points say load (%1RM) looks more influential than proximity for maximal strength. Model fit was modest, and the authors flag that the RIR estimation limits precision.
- 검증 결과: This is a set of exploratory multilevel meta-regressions in which RIR was estimated from each study's description. All models were adjusted for load, method of volume equating, duration and training status. Strength: 243 effects from 55 studies; RIR slope 0.003 SMC (95% CI -0.012 to 0.018), so the relationship is negligible. Hypertrophy: 140 effects from 26 studies; slope -0.019 (-0.035 to -0.004). A sensitivity model excluding effects estimated at 10 or more RIR (137 effects) gave -0.023 (-0.042 to -0.004). Model fit was modest. Key Points: 'Load (% of 1RM) seems to be a more influential training variable for maximal strength'.
- 검증 메모: PubMed returned a CAPTCHA. Verified from the PubMed text via Exa and from the accepted manuscript (Abertay repository) and SportRxiv preprint. The -0.019 to -0.023 range is the main model plus the sensitivity model. Prediction intervals are very wide (about -0.55 to +0.51), so individual responses vary a lot.
- REF5 관련성: This is the main evidence that REF5's non-failure, slowdown-terminated sets do not cost much strength. It also shows the cost lands on hypertrophy, mainly in the volume slots that sit far from failure, and that the load REF5 uses matters more than how close it gets to failure.
- 조사 당시 표기: Robinson ZP, Pelland JC, Remmert JF, Refalo MC, Jukic I, Steele J, Zourdos MC. Exploring the Dose-Response Relationship Between Estimated Resistance Training Proximity to Failure, Strength Gain, and Muscle Hypertrophy: A Series of Meta-Regressions. Sports Med. 2024 Sep;54(9). doi:10.1007/s40279-024-02069-2
- 조사 당시 URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38970765/

### PA-02 · confirmed

**Robinson Z, Macarilla C, Juber M, Cerminaro R, Benitez B, et al. Int J Strength Cond. 2025;5(1)**

- RCT · 2025 · https://journal.iusca.org/index.php/Journal/article/view/393
- 조사 요약: 38 trained men, 8 weeks, four groups: 4-6 RIR, 1-3 RIR, 0-3 RIR (last set to failure) and 0 RIR. Bench 1RM gains: 4-6 RIR +9.05 kg (95% CI 6.31-11.8), 1-3 RIR +9.72 kg, 0-3 RIR +5.07 kg, 0 RIR +0.71 kg. Squat gains: 4-6 RIR +13.79 kg, 1-3 RIR +18.05 kg, with wide CIs that overlap. The squat data for the failure groups were hard to interpret because those protocols were poorly feasible. Hypertrophy was unclear.
- 검증 결과: 38 trained men in 4 groups (4-6 RIR, 1-3 RIR, 0-3 RIR, 0 RIR), 8 weeks, 3 sessions per week. Bench gains: +9.05 kg (6.31-11.8), +9.72 kg, +5.07 kg and +0.71 kg. Squat gains: 4-6 RIR +13.79 kg (7.43-20.28) and 1-3 RIR +18.05 kg (12.12-23.88). Squat data for the failure groups were hard to interpret because those protocols were poorly feasible. Hypertrophy was unclear, and fatigue indices were largely similar. Full text: velocity data indicate the 4-6 RIR group actually trained at about 8 RIR in the squat and about 6 RIR on bench. RIR was manipulated only for squat and bench; accessories (OHP, row, curls and others) were done at 2 RIR.
- 검증 메모: The list has 15 authors (Robinson ... Helms, Zourdos). The finding is accurate. The added detail strengthens the high-RIR case: squat sets ending about 8 RIR still gained +13.8 kg. The squat point estimate favored 1-3 RIR by about 4 kg, though the CIs overlap.
- REF5 관련성: This is direct RCT support that sets ending at about 2-6 RIR give strength gains comparable to closer-to-failure training in trained men. Training to failure may give worse results. It supports REF5's rule against grinding.
- 조사 당시 표기: Robinson Z, Macarilla C, Juber M, Cerminaro R, Benitez B, et al. The Effect of Resistance Training Proximity to Failure on Muscular Adaptations and Longitudinal Fatigue in Trained Men. International Journal of Strength and Conditioning. 2025

### PA-03 · confirmed

**Ruple BA, Plotkin DL, Smith MA, et al. Physiol Rep. 2023;11(9):e15679**

- RCT · 2023 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37144554/
- 조사 요약: 19 trained adults with a squat:BM ratio of about 1.6 trained squat, bench and deadlift for 5 weeks plus a deload week, ending sets at either 0-1 RIR or 4-6 RIR. Total volume did not differ (p=0.222). The squat, bench and deadlift 1RMs and VL mCSA showed no group-by-time interactions. The low-RIR group showed changes in motor-unit firing rate and a non-significant larger rise in isometric torque.
- 검증 결과: 19 trained adults (11 men, 8 women; squat:body-mass ratio 1.60 ± 0.33) trained squat, bench and deadlift for 5 weeks with sets ending at 0-1 RIR or 4-6 RIR, then had a deload week 6. Total volume did not differ (p=0.222). The squat, bench and deadlift 1RMs and VL mCSA showed no condition-by-time interactions. The low-RIR group showed changes in the slope and intercept of motor-unit firing rate.
- 검증 메모: Verified from the Wiley full text. I could not verify the claim of a 'non-significant larger rise in isometric torque' from the abstract. The sample was mixed-sex, not men only.
- REF5 관련성: These lifters are similar to the REF5 athlete. Stopping 4-6 reps short on the same lifts produced the same 1RM gains over a short block. This supports REF5's high-RIR operating zone for strength.
- 조사 당시 표기: Ruple BA, Plotkin DL, Smith MA, Godwin JS, Sexton CL, McIntosh MC, et al. The effects of resistance training to near failure on strength, hypertrophy, and motor unit adaptations in previously trained adults. Physiol Rep. 2023;11(9):e15679

### PA-04 · confirmed

**Martikainen O, Niiranen H, Rytkonen T, Schoenfeld BJ, Ahtiainen JP, (Hulmi JJ) et al. J Sci Sport Exerc. 2025**

- RCT · 2025 · https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42978-025-00338-8
- 조사 요약: Trained men and women trained for 10 weeks with RIR stepping 4 to 1 versus a constant 1 RIR. The groups did not differ: squat 1RM +9.8% vs +9.2%, bench +7.0% vs +9.6%, VL CSA +6.5% vs +5.5%. Session RPE was lower in the RIR 1-4 group. Squat RIR3 estimation error improved from -1.5 to -0.6 reps with practice.
- 검증 결과: 31 trained men and women completed the study (39 were recruited). It ran 10 weeks as two 5-week blocks, each with a deload in week 5. RIR stepped 4→1 in the RIR1-4 group and stayed at 1 in the RIR1 group. Squat 1RM: +9.8% vs +9.2%. Bench: +7.0% vs +9.6%. VL CSA: +6.5% vs +5.5%. None of these interactions were significant. Session RPE was lower in RIR1-4 (6.9 vs 7.7). In RIR1-4, squat RIR3 error improved from -1.5 to -0.6 reps and bench from -1.1 to -0.1.
- 검증 메모: The comparison is RIR 1-4 against RIR 1, not 4-6 RIR against failure. The deload weeks were part of the protocol.
- REF5 관련성: Easier average proximity did not reduce strength gains and gave lower session strain, which fits REF5's session RPE target of about 7-8. It also shows that proximity estimation is a skill that improves with practice, which matters for how reliable the 'obvious slowdown' call is.
- 조사 당시 표기: Martikainen O, Niiranen H, Rytkonen T, Schoenfeld BJ, Ahtiainen JP. Influence of Varying Proximity-to-Failure on Muscular Adaptations and Repetitions-in-Reserve Estimation Accuracy in Resistance-Trained Individuals. Journal of Science in Sport and Exercise. 2025

### PA-05 · confirmed

**Grgic J, Schoenfeld BJ, Orazem J, Sabol F. J Sport Health Sci. 2022;11(2):202-211**

- meta-analysis · 2022 · https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2021.01.007
- 조사 요약: 15 studies. Failure vs non-failure showed no difference for strength (ES -0.09, 95% CI -0.22 to 0.05) or hypertrophy (ES 0.22, -0.11 to 0.55). When volume was not equated, non-failure favored strength (ES -0.32, -0.57 to -0.07). In the trained subgroup, failure gave a small hypertrophy advantage (ES 0.15, 0.03-0.26).
- 검증 결과: 15 studies. Strength ES -0.09 (-0.22 to 0.05). Hypertrophy ES 0.22 (-0.11 to 0.55). With volume not equated, non-failure was favored for strength, ES -0.32 (-0.57 to -0.07). In the trained subgroup, failure was favored for hypertrophy, ES 0.15 (0.03 to 0.26).
- 검증 메모: Verified via the Europe PMC abstract. Ruple 2023 notes that the trained subgroup contained only 2 studies.
- REF5 관련성: Supports REF5's 'no intentional failure' rule for strength. Flags a small hypertrophy trade-off in trained lifters.
- 조사 당시 표기: Grgic J, Schoenfeld BJ, Orazem J, Sabol F. Effects of resistance training performed to repetition failure or non-failure on muscular strength and hypertrophy: A systematic review and meta-analysis. J Sport Health Sci. 2022;11(2):202-211

### PA-06 · confirmed

**Vieira AF, Umpierre D, Teodoro JL, et al. J Strength Cond Res. 2021;35(4):1165-1175**

- meta-analysis · 2021 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33555822/
- 조사 요약: 13 studies. Maximal strength did not differ overall (SMD -0.08, p=0.642). Non-failure was better for strength when volumes were not equalized (SMD -0.34, p=0.048) and for power (SMD -0.61, p=0.025). Failure was better for hypertrophy overall (SMD 0.75) but not when volume was equalized.
- 검증 결과: 13 studies. Strength overall SMD -0.08 (p=0.642). Non-equalized volume favored non-failure for strength (SMD -0.34, p=0.048) and for power (SMD -0.61, p=0.025). Failure was better for hypertrophy overall (SMD 0.75, p=0.005), with no difference when volume was equalized.
- REF5 관련성: Agrees with Grgic: stopping short is at least as good for strength and power. The hypertrophy advantage of failure largely reflects extra volume, which REF5 does not add.
- 조사 당시 표기: Vieira AF, Umpierre D, Teodoro JL, Lisboa SC, Baroni BM, Izquierdo M, Cadore EL. Effects of Resistance Training Performed to Failure or Not to Failure on Muscle Strength, Hypertrophy, and Power Output: A Systematic Review With Meta-Analysis. J Strength Cond Res. 2021;35(4):1165-1175

### PA-07 · confirmed

**Refalo MC, Helms ER, Trexler ET, Hamilton DL, Fyfe JJ. Sports Med. 2023;53(3):649-665**

- meta-analysis · 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y
- 조사 요약: 15 studies. Momentary failure vs non-failure for hypertrophy: ES 0.12 (-0.13 to 0.37), not significant. High (>25%) vs moderate (20-25%) velocity loss: ES 0.08 (-0.16 to 0.32). Low velocity loss (<20%) had smaller within-group hypertrophy (ES 0.20) than moderate (0.39) or high (0.42). The relationship looks non-linear.
- 검증 결과: 15 studies. Momentary failure vs non-failure: ES 0.12 (-0.13 to 0.37), p=0.343. High vs moderate VL: ES 0.08 (-0.16 to 0.32). Within-group pre-post ES: low VL (<20%) 0.20 (-0.02 to 0.41, p=0.072), moderate 0.39, high 0.42. The authors propose a non-linear relationship. Omitted from the summary: across any definition of set failure vs non-failure there was a trivial advantage for failure, ES 0.19 (0.00 to 0.37), p=0.045.
- 검증 메모: The low-VL estimate rests on only 6 ESs from 3 studies and was never formally compared, because low VL was excluded from the comparative model. Treat 'very early stopping grows less' as suggestive, not established.
- REF5 관련성: Sets do not need to reach failure for hypertrophy, but sets stopped very early (low velocity loss, many RIR) grow less. REF5's volume slots probably fall in that 'very early' zone.
- 조사 당시 표기: Refalo MC, Helms ER, Trexler ET, Hamilton DL, Fyfe JJ. Influence of Resistance Training Proximity-to-Failure on Skeletal Muscle Hypertrophy: A Systematic Review with Meta-analysis. Sports Med. 2023;53(3):649-665

### PA-08 · confirmed

**Refalo MC, Helms ER, Robinson ZP, Hamilton DL, Fyfe JJ. J Sports Sci. 2024;42(1):85-101**

- RCT · 2024 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38393985/
- 조사 요약: Within-subject unilateral leg press and leg extension, 18 trained participants, 8 weeks. Quadriceps thickness gains were the same for failure (+0.181 cm, +6.96%) and 1-2 RIR (+0.182 cm, +6.98%). Failure caused more velocity and repetition loss. Participants predicted RIR within about 1 rep.
- 검증 결과: 18 trained participants (12 men, 6 women), within-subject design, leg press to 2 RIR and leg extension to 1 RIR vs failure, 2 sessions per week for 8 weeks. Quadriceps thickness +0.181 cm (+6.96%) with failure vs +0.182 cm (+6.98%) with RIR. Failure produced more velocity and repetition loss. Absolute RIR accuracy was under 1 rep for both the 1-RIR and 3-RIR targets.
- 검증 메모: Machine exercises only.
- REF5 관련성: Stopping 1-2 reps short gives full hypertrophy, but this does not cover REF5's 5-9 RIR volume sets. Training here was machine-based, not barbell.
- 조사 당시 표기: Refalo MC, Helms ER, Robinson ZP, Hamilton DL, Fyfe JJ. Similar muscle hypertrophy following eight weeks of resistance training to momentary muscular failure or with repetitions-in-reserve in resistance-trained individuals. J Sports Sci. 2024;42(1):85-101

### PA-09 · confirmed

**Refalo MC, Helms ER, Hamilton DL, Fyfe JJ. Sports Med Open. 2023;9:10**

- RCT · 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1186/s40798-023-00554-y
- 조사 요약: Crossover with 24 trained participants doing 6 sets of bench at 75% 1RM. Velocity loss at 4 min post: failure -25%, 1-RIR -13%, 3-RIR -8%. At 24 h: failure -3%, 1-RIR -3%, 3-RIR +2%. All recovered by 48 h. Discomfort, soreness and perceived recovery all worsened as proximity neared.
- 검증 결과: Crossover with 24 trained participants (12 men, 12 women), 6 sets of bench at 75% 1RM. Velocity at 4 min post: failure -25%, 1-RIR -13%, 3-RIR -8%. At 24 h: -3%, -3% and +2%. Between-protocol differences had diminished by 48 h. Perceptual responses worsened as sets came closer to failure.
- 검증 메모: Bench only. Applying the 24 h recovery result to squat is extrapolation.
- REF5 관련성: Sets ending about 3 reps short leave the lifter fully recovered within 24 h. This supports REF5's no-failure stance for an athlete with poor recovery (C2) and clustered training days (C1).
- 조사 당시 표기: Refalo MC, Helms ER, Hamilton DL, Fyfe JJ. Influence of Resistance Training Proximity-to-Failure, Determined by Repetitions-in-Reserve, on Neuromuscular Fatigue in Resistance-Trained Males and Females. Sports Med Open. 2023;9:10

### PA-10 · confirmed

**Moran-Navarro R, Perez CE, Mora-Rodriguez R, et al. Eur J Appl Physiol. 2017;117(12):2387-2399**

- RCT · 2017 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28965198/
- 조사 요약: Trained men did bench and full squat as 3x5(10), 6x5(10) or 3x10(10). Failure caused a larger acute drop in CMJ and velocity. Recovery of the non-failure protocols, even the volume-matched 6x5, was much faster between 24 and 48 h. Failure delays recovery by 24-48 h.
- 검증 결과: 10 trained men did bench and full squat as 3x5(10), 6x5(10) or 3x10(10). Failure caused larger acute drops in CMJ and velocity than even the volume-matched 6x5. The non-failure protocols recovered significantly faster between 24 and 48 h. Failure slowed recovery by up to 24-48 h.
- REF5 관련성: Supports pairing no-failure sets with REF5's density rules (≥48 h between hard squats, forced micro sessions after back-to-back days).
- 조사 당시 표기: Moran-Navarro R, Perez CE, Mora-Rodriguez R, de la Cruz-Sanchez E, Gonzalez-Badillo JJ, Sanchez-Medina L, Pallares JG. Time course of recovery following resistance training leading or not to failure. Eur J Appl Physiol. 2017;117(12):2387-2399

### PA-11 · confirmed

**Pareja-Blanco F, Rodriguez-Rosell D, Sanchez-Medina L, et al. Clin Physiol Funct Imaging. 2017;37(6):630-639**

- RCT · 2017 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26970332/
- 조사 요약: 3x6 vs 3x12 at a 70% 1RM load where 12 reps was the maximum. Within-set velocity loss: squat 20% vs 44%, bench 26% vs 65%. Doing half the possible reps cut fatigue and sped up recovery: CMJ was depressed up to 48 h only after the failure protocol, and CK, cortisol and prolactin rose more after failure.
- 검증 결과: 10 men did 3x6[12] vs 3x12[12] at 70% 1RM. Within-set VL: bench 26% vs 65%, squat 20% vs 44%. CMJ was depressed up to 48 h after the failure protocol only. Cortisol, prolactin and CK rose more after failure. Halving the possible reps minimized fatigue.
- 검증 메모: This is an acute within-subject comparison, not a training RCT. The claim that 20% VL is hard to notice by feel is an inference supported by Sindiani 2020 and Shaw 2023, not by this paper.
- REF5 관련성: Gives a physiological anchor for REF5's sets. Doing about half the possible reps produces only about 20% velocity loss in the squat, which is hard to notice by feel. That suggests most REF5 sets end long before an 'obvious slowdown' appears.
- 조사 당시 표기: Pareja-Blanco F, Rodriguez-Rosell D, Sanchez-Medina L, Ribas-Serna J, Lopez-Lopez C, Mora-Custodio R, et al. Acute and delayed response to resistance exercise leading or not leading to muscle failure. Clin Physiol Funct Imaging. 2017;37(6):630-639

### PA-12 · confirmed

**Pareja-Blanco F, Rodriguez-Rosell D, Sanchez-Medina L, et al. Scand J Med Sci Sports. 2017;27(7):724-735**

- RCT · 2017 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27038416/
- 조사 요약: 8 weeks of squat with VL20 vs VL40. Squat 1RM gains were similar even though VL20 did 40% fewer reps. VL20 improved CMJ more (9.5% vs 3.5%). VL40 gave more VL/VI hypertrophy but reduced the MHC IIX percentage. In the VL40 condition, about 56% of sets reached failure.
- 검증 결과: 22 young men trained the squat for 8 weeks at VL20 (n=12) or VL40 (n=10). Squat strength gains were similar, although VL20 did 40% fewer reps. CMJ: +9.5% vs +3.5%. VL40 gave more VL/VI hypertrophy and reduced MHC IIX. Full text: in VL40, 56.3% of sets reached failure.
- 검증 메모: The squat was performed in a Smith machine (Larsen 2021, Table 2). Per Larsen 2021, 1RM rose +17.6% vs +13.5% (within-group ES 1.53 vs 0.79).
- REF5 관련성: Stopping at a moderate slowdown is as good for squat strength as training near failure, and is more time- and fatigue-efficient. This is the closest physiological analogue to REF5's 'first obvious slowdown' rule.
- 조사 당시 표기: Pareja-Blanco F, Rodriguez-Rosell D, Sanchez-Medina L, Sanchis-Moysi J, Dorado C, Mora-Custodio R, et al. Effects of velocity loss during resistance training on athletic performance, strength gains and muscle adaptations. Scand J Med Sci Sports. 2017;27(7):724-735

### PA-13 · confirmed

**Pareja-Blanco F, Alcazar J, Cornejo-Daza PJ, et al. Scand J Med Sci Sports. 2020;30(11):2154-2166**

- RCT · 2020 · https://doi.org/10.1111/sms.13775
- 조사 요약: 64 trained men, 8 weeks of bench at VL0, VL15, VL25 or VL50. Dynamic strength showed no between-group differences; all groups improved. VL50 increased pectoralis CSA more than VL0. Lower thresholds favored neural and RFD adaptations.

- REF5 관련성: Strength is robust across stopping points, including very early ones. Hypertrophy suffers when sets stop very early, which is relevant to the BP volume 2x5 at about 69% and the other volume slots.
- 조사 당시 표기: Pareja-Blanco F, Alcazar J, Cornejo-Daza PJ, Sanchez-Valdepenas J, Rodriguez-Lopez C, Hidalgo-de Mora J, et al. Effects of velocity loss in the bench press exercise on strength gains, neuromuscular adaptations, and muscle hypertrophy. Scand J Med Sci Sports. 2020;30(11):2154-2166

### PA-14 · corrected

**Pareja-Blanco F, Sanchez-Valdepenas J, Cornejo-Daza PJ, Rodiles-Guerrero L. Med Sci Sports Exerc. 2026 (online 2026-03-24)**

- RCT · 2026 · https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41872733/
- 조사 요약: 158 trained men in 12 groups, 8 weeks of bench: 3 intensity bands (40-55%, 55-70%, 70-85% 1RM) crossed with 4 VL thresholds (0%, 15%, 25%, 50%). The 70-85% 1RM band gave the largest gains in CSA, 1RM and submaximal velocity. VL25 gave the largest 1RM gain. VL50 gave the most CSA and reps to failure. VL0 was weakest.
- 검증 결과: 158 trained men in 12 groups (3 intensity bands × VL0/15/25/50) did 3 sets of bench twice a week for 8 weeks. The 70-85% band gave the greatest gains in CSA, 1RM, submaximal velocity and MNR. VL25 gave the greatest 1RM gain and was significantly greater than VL0. VL50 gave the greatest CSA (more than VL0 and VL15) and MNR. In VL0 groups each set was a single rep. An ACSM author commentary describes the strength-vs-fatigue relationship at a given load as an inverted U.
- 검증 메모: Correction: there are only 4 authors, so 'et al.' is wrong. 'VL0 was weakest' is only partly supported: VL25 > VL0 for 1RM, and VL50 > VL0 and VL15 for CSA. Mapping REF5's BP focus 3x3 to VL25 or below is extrapolation. Participants' bench relative 1RM was about 0.93 BW.
- REF5 관련성: This is the most recent large trial. For 1RM it favors heavy loads (70-85%) stopped at moderate fatigue, about 40-50% of possible reps. REF5's BP focus 3x3 at about 82-84% of REF probably sits at or below VL25. The BP volume 2x5 at about 69% is lighter than the best band.
- 조사 당시 표기: Pareja-Blanco F, Sanchez-Valdepenas J, Cornejo-Daza PJ, Rodiles-Guerrero L, et al. Optimizing Strength and Hypertrophy: The Combined Effect of Intensity and Velocity Loss Thresholds in Bench Press Training. Med Sci Sports Exerc. 2026 (online 2026-03-24)

### PA-15 · confirmed

**Jukic I, Perez Castilla A, Garcia Ramos A, Van Hooren B, McGuigan MR, Helms ER. Sports Med. 2023;53(1):177-214**

- meta-analysis · 2023 · https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01754-4
- 조사 요약: 18 acute and 19 longitudinal studies. The VL threshold did not affect gains in strength or muscular endurance. Higher VL increased hypertrophy (b=0.006 per % VL, 95% CI 0.001-0.012) but impaired CMJ, sprint and submaximal-velocity outcomes. Exercise, load and training history moderate how velocity loss maps to reps.
- 검증 결과: 18 acute and 19 longitudinal studies. VL did not influence strength or muscle-endurance gains. Higher VL increased hypertrophy (b=0.006; 95% CI 0.001-0.012) and impaired CMJ (b=-0.040), sprint and submaximal velocity. Exercise, load and individual characteristics moderated these relationships.
- 검증 메모: Published online in 2022.
- REF5 관련성: Strength is insensitive to where a set is stopped. Hypertrophy is not. The squat-vs-bench mapping differences mean the same 'obvious slowdown' can sit at a different RIR in each lift.
- 조사 당시 표기: Jukic I, Perez Castilla A, Garcia Ramos A, Van Hooren B, McGuigan MR, Helms ER. The Acute and Chronic Effects of Implementing Velocity Loss Thresholds During Resistance Training: A Systematic Review, Meta-Analysis, and Critical Evaluation of the Literature. Sports Med. 2023;53(1):177-214

### PA-16 · corrected

## REF5 함의: 검증에서 약화

- The SQ RIR estimates (H3 3x3 at about 82% → about 3-4 RIR; H2 3x2 at about 87% → about 2-3 RIR) are not reproduced by Nuzzo 2024 alone. Its main model (about 15 reps at 70%, about 5 at 90%) implies about 5 RIR for H3 and about 4 RIR for H2. The lower figures depend entirely on the athlete's single 92.5x3 RPE 9-10 data point. Zourdos 2016 does not back that adjustment: its '5-7 reps at 75%' came from perceived RIR after a single rep, and the authors attribute it to estimation difficulty. Report the hard-slot RIR as roughly 2-5 with wide uncertainty, not 2-3.
- 'Slowdown is a valid proximity proxy (R² 0.93-0.97 SQ/BP)' needs a caveat. Those fits come from the González-Badillo/Pareja-Blanco lab, which used a Smith machine (confirmed for Moran-Navarro 2019 and Pareja-Blanco 2017). Jukic 2023 EJAP found the VL-%reps relationship unreliable between sessions in the free-weight back squat (prediction errors >10%). For REF5's free-weight squat, slowdown is a coarser proxy than the R² values suggest. Sanchez-Moreno's pull-up R² 0.88 came from bodyweight sets, not heavy weighted triples.
- 'No velocity device: the evidence CONTRADICTS that a binary rule loses little information' is overstated for the free-weight squat. Jukic 2024's individual RIR-velocity models predicted RIR with mean error under 2 reps. That is not clearly better than trained lifters' subjective RIR accuracy (about 0.65-1.2 reps). Moran-Navarro 2019's reliable stopping velocities are Smith-machine data, and its claim of superiority over perceived exertion was not tested. Jukic 2023 EJAP says VL-based prescription offers no benefit over costless methods in the free-weight squat. The binary rule still discards graded information, but the marginal value of a device is uncertain, not clearly large.
- Volume-slot implication: Androulakis-Korakakis 2020 is misused as support that very early stopping still raises 1RM, because its minimum-dose protocols went to failure. Better support: Robinson 2025 (the nominal 4-6 RIR group trained at about 8 RIR in squat and gained +13.8 kg) and Pareja-Blanco 2020 (VL0 bench still improved). On the hypertrophy side, Refalo 2023's low-VL estimate (ES 0.20, CI -0.02 to 0.41) rests on 6 ESs from 3 studies and was not formally compared. Pareja-Blanco 2026's VL0 groups did 1 rep per set. 'Contradicts that they contribute much hypertrophy' is directionally right but weaker than stated.
- Autoregulation evidence: describing Huang 2025 as 'low certainty' is the researcher's own judgement, not the authors'. The authors did no GRADE/CINeMA rating and concluded autoregulation is significantly better. The network has only 19 RCTs (3 RPE studies, ages 10-30). Helms 2018 shows no 'drift' to about RPE 6 over time: it reports 8-week averages (squat 6.5, bench 5.8), and squat relative intensity did not differ between groups (79.7% vs 78.7%). The under-loading-drift argument therefore rests mainly on Graham & Cleather 2021 (squat) and Helms 2018 (bench only). Mann 2010 is weak support: not volume-matched, bench baselines 134 vs 154 kg, estimated squat 1RM.
- A larger step (+5 kg) after a clean window remains plausible, but its evidence is thinner than presented, for the reasons above. Helms 2018 squat and Mann 2010 add little. No study tests step-size changes inside a fixed-increment program.
- 'Stopping at moderate VL (20-30%) is optimal for 1RM' is partly weakened. Zhang 2023's values are model estimates (likely % gain), not ES. Hickmott 2022's ≤25% strength advantage appeared only when additional exercises were performed. Pareja-Blanco 2020 and Myrholt 2023 found no between-group strength differences. Moderate VL is at best marginally better, and evidence for strength equivalence across stopping points is stronger than evidence for a single optimum.
- Uncertainty statement corrections: Pareja-Blanco 2026 has exactly 4 authors, so no further list exists to check. Robinson 2025 has 15 authors and trained 3 times a week, with accessories to 2 RIR. Sindiani 2020's improvement came from practice between sessions, and the abstract does not say a device was used for feedback; the claim that feedback-based learning required a device is unverified (Lazarus et al. was not checked).

## REF5 함의: 조사 단계 원안

- §11.1 'stop before ROM/technique loss, no extra reps, no intentional failure': SUPPORTED for strength. Meta-analyses (Grgic 2022, Vieira 2021, Robinson 2024, Jukic 2023) and RCTs in trained lifters show strength gains are similar from about 0-1 RIR out to 4-6 RIR. Examples: Ruple 2023 (0-1 vs 4-6 RIR) and Robinson 2025 (4-6 RIR ≈ 1-3 RIR; 0 RIR inferior on bench). Martikainen 2025 found no difference between varying RIR 1-4 and constant RIR 1. Recovery data (Moran-Navarro 2017, Pareja-Blanco 2017 CPFI, Refalo 2023 SMO) also support the rule for an athlete with poor recovery (C2) and clustered training days (C1). This is direct evidence, though the trials last only 5-12 weeks.
- Direct answer to 'is ~2-4 RIR enough for intermediates?': yes for strength over the time frames studied. The evidence is direct and consistent: even 4-6 RIR matched closer-to-failure training over 5-10 weeks. Load (%1RM) is the bigger lever (Currier 2023: ≥80% 1RM; Robinson 2024: load more influential). REF5's hard SQ (H3 at about 82%, H2 at about 87% of the stated 1RM) and BP/PULL focus slots (about 82-84% of control REF) sit in the strength-optimal load band. These are estimates: by interpolating Nuzzo 2024 and the athlete's own 92.5x3 at RPE 9-10, SQ H3 3x3 leaves about 3-4 RIR and H2 about 2-3 RIR. Extrapolation; individual SD is about 2.5 reps.
- The athlete's adopted diagnosis ('never had frequency + proximity + recoverable volume') is partly CONTRADICTED on proximity. The evidence says strength is insensitive to proximity across a wide RIR range once load is controlled. The Tactical Barbell (TB) and 5x5 stalls were therefore more likely due to load not tracking capacity, or to volume, frequency and recovery, than to too little proximity. Practical consequence: if REF5 later stalls, the evidence does not favor going closer to failure as the first fix. This is consistent with the athlete's pre-registered rule that a stall means stimulus or recovery is short, not that judgment should be relaxed.
- §11.1 'first obvious slowdown counts, then the set ends' together with 'ambiguous → not a slowdown': PARTLY SUPPORTED in principle. Slowdown is a valid proxy for proximity: velocity loss predicts the percentage of reps done with R² 0.93-0.97 for SQ/BP and 0.88 for pull-ups. Stopping at moderate velocity loss (about 20-30%) is optimal or equivalent for 1RM (Hickmott 2022, Zhang 2023, Pareja-Blanco 2017/2020/2026). Judging it by feel is noisy, though. Perception error is about 1 rep on bench and worse at heavier loads (Dello Iacono 2023). Squat changes are under-perceived 4.2 times more often (Sindiani 2020). Deadlift perception is near chance (Shaw 2023). Inference (extrapolation): REF5's HOLD trigger probably fires only at large velocity loss, roughly ≤1-3 RIR in SQ at 80-87%. A PASS therefore means 'at least about 2 RIR left', not 'close to capacity'. The 'ambiguous → not a slowdown' tie-break strengthens this bias. The trigger is also not equally strict across lifts: the same slowdown means fewer RIR left in SQ than in BP (Rodriguez-Rosell 2020).
- §11.3 PASS/HOLD/FAIL plus the window controller (§12): the evidence is SILENT on window-based binary rep-deficit judges; there is no validation study. The nearest analogues are APRE, RPE/RIR and VBT. Pooled, these give small and uncertain advantages over fixed loading (Hickmott 2022 MD +2.07 kg, ns; Huang 2025 NMA, low certainty). Where advantages appear, they come from load tracking rising capacity: Graham & Cleather 2021, BS +10.8% vs +7.1% at higher training intensity; Helms 2018, where the %1RM group drifted to about RPE 6. Structurally, REF5's judge detects 'too heavy' (HOLD/FAIL) but never 'too light'. With 28 sessions and 0 HOLD/FAIL, the controller has not yet engaged, so REF5 has behaved as fixed linear progression at +2.5 kg per window. REF5 does prevent the upward drift to grinding that happened in TB (good, and consistent with the athlete's history). It cannot detect the opposite under-loading drift, the mirror image, in which %1RM falls if strength rises faster than the window cadence.
- Logging-only option (no protocol change, no change to judgment criteria; a §26-style proposal if adopted): record a 3-level observation tag per focus/hard exercise, 'no slowdown / ambiguous slowdown / clear slowdown'. Only 'clear' would keep its current HOLD effect; 'ambiguous' would stay a PASS exactly as §11.1 requires. Rationale: it recovers part of the information the binary rule discards (spec §22.4 #7) and gives the first data able to tell under-loading (consistently no slowdown) from headroom shrinking (more ambiguous tags) before any HOLD appears. Evidence: perception of velocity and RIR is coarse but improves with practice and feedback (Sindiani 2020; Lazarus et al. as cited in Dello Iacono 2023; Martikainen 2025), so the tag gets more reliable over time. Support for doing this is indirect.
- Only if that tag data showed sustained 'no slowdown' across a whole window, one possible later single-element change: a larger step (for example +5 kg) for windows that close with 0 HOLD and 0 ambiguous tags. The FAIL 0 / HOLD ≤1 / veto criteria stay exactly as they are. Evidence is indirect: Graham & Cleather 2021, Helms 2018, Mann 2010 and Huang 2025 show that letting load catch up with capacity speeds strength gains. The downside is bounded because REF5's existing 2-consecutive-FAIL rule (-2.5 kg) catches overshoot. Unknown: whether the faster loading aggravates the quadriceps-tendon symptoms. This is not recommended now; at most it would be the next experiment after the logging change, never at the same time.
- Volume slots (SQ V 3x5 at about 70%, BP volume 2x5 at about 69%, DL 2x4 at about 75% of REF, OHP 2x6 at about 65% of REF, micro 1x5/1x6): based on reps-to-failure tables these probably end about 5-9+ reps short (extrapolation; REFs are not 1RMs). For strength and skill practice, the evidence SUPPORTS them, since very early stopping still raises 1RM (Pareja-Blanco 2020 VL0/VL15; Androulakis-Korakakis 2020). As hypertrophy stimuli, the evidence CONTRADICTS assuming they contribute much: Robinson 2024 shows a negative RIR slope for hypertrophy, Hickmott 2022 found >25% VL beat ≤20% for CSA, Pareja-Blanco 2020 found VL50 beat VL0 for CSA, and Refalo 2023 found low velocity loss gave the smallest effect. For a multi-year 2.5xBW goal pursued during a 75→70 kg cut, added muscle will matter eventually (extrapolation; Mattocks 2017 shows strength and size dissociate only in the short term). This is a cost of C5/no AMRAP that the program cannot see. It should be reviewed through the volume and stimulus audit, not by changing set termination.
- C10 (no RIR): evidence is MIXED and the cost is modest. Trained lifters judge 1-3 RIR on bench with about 0.65 reps absolute error (Refalo 2024), but error grows far from failure: about 1.1-1.2 reps at 4-5 RIR (Remmert 2023), with about 1 rep of underprediction overall (Halperin 2022). REF5 operates at 3-6+ RIR, where RIR would itself be imprecise, so rejecting RIR loses less resolution than it might seem. The binary rule removes ambiguity the athlete dislikes, at the cost of graded information near the threshold. The judgment criteria do not need to change for this.
- No velocity device: the evidence CONTRADICTS the idea that a binary rule loses little information. Individual RIR-velocity profiles for the free-weight back squat predict RIR within <2 reps (Jukic 2024). Stopping velocities are load-independent and reliable, with CV 4-8% (Moran-Navarro 2019), and VBT gave the most accurate prescription (Cowley 2025). REF5 deliberately gives this up. Its spec already lists the cost (§22.4 #7). The cost can be stated but should not be recommended against the user's principle.
- Session RPE target about 7-8 and the anti-grinding rule: SUPPORTED. Robinson 2025 found 0 RIR inferior on bench, and Pareja-Blanco 2017 found VL40 preserved strength no better than VL20 while lowering MHC IIX. The coaching consensus agrees: Stronger by Science (2025) recommends RPE 5-8 for most strength work. One coaching caveat (RTS): never training near-maximal efforts may leave 'grinding' skill and 1RM expression undeveloped (Mattocks 2017, test practice). This matters only if G1 is ever verified by a true max, and C9 forbids testing.
- §11.3 HOLD handling and the pre-registered reading of HOLDs: SUPPORTED with a caveat. Because squat slowdowns are under-perceived and perception varies from session to session (Sindiani 2020; Dello Iacono 2023), an isolated HOLD carries measurement noise as well as real fatigue. The design of allowing at most one HOLD per 4- or 6-exposure window already absorbs this, so no relaxation is needed. When interpreting a low success rate, note that clustered HOLDs are a far stronger signal than a single one.
- DL rule (4 normal exposures, all PASS) and the slowdown rule applied to DL: the evidence SUGGESTS the slowdown component adds little information for the deadlift. Perceiving deadlift velocity loss is near chance (Shaw 2023), and DL at about 75% of REF probably leaves 6 or more RIR. DL progression therefore effectively depends on rep completion and the SQ-based cap.
- PULL (weighted pull-up) focus 3x3 at about 82-83% of the last total 1RM: velocity loss is a valid proxy for proximity in pull-ups (Sanchez-Moreno 2017), so the slowdown rule is physiologically meaningful there, and the evidence SUPPORTS it. The 1RM velocity is very low (about 0.20 m/s), so a visible slowdown may appear well before failure. Because bodyweight changes alter relative load, an unmeasured, defaulted 75 kg log entry weakens the judge for PULL. The OAP rung judgments have no evidence base (SILENT).

## 불확실성

- Every RCT comparing proximity levels lasts 5-12 weeks and mostly involves men in their 20s with intermediate training status. None covers a multi-year goal, a lifter in a concurrent caloric deficit, high-bar squat specifically, or someone who also climbs. Applying short-term equivalence to a 175 kg / 2.5xBW multi-year target is extrapolation.
- Robinson 2024 estimated RIR after the fact from study descriptions, and its models fit only modestly. Robinson 2025 is a small IJSC trial (n=38 across 4 arms) in which the failure arms were hard to carry out. Martikainen 2025 and Ruple 2023 are small too (about 15-19 per comparison). The non-significant strength differences rule out large effects but not small ones.
- No study validates REF5's specific instrument: a binary 'first obvious slowdown' call combined with a rep-deficit PASS/HOLD/FAIL and a 4- or 6-exposure window. The mapping 'PASS ≈ ≥2 RIR, HOLD fires at about 1-3 RIR in SQ' is inferred from perception and velocity-loss studies. Those mostly used bench, Smith machine or moderate loads (50-70% 1RM), not free-weight high-bar sets of 2-3 reps at 80-87%.
- The athlete's true 1RMs are unknown. The control REFs (SQ 104, BP 101, PULL 108, DL 100, OHP 50) are explicitly not 1RMs, and no max test is allowed. All RIR and %1RM estimates for REF5 slots therefore carry about ±2-3 reps of uncertainty (reps-to-failure SD about 2.5 reps at 80% in Nuzzo 2024, CV 26-34% for squat in Rodriguez-Rosell 2020). Strength may have risen since the 92.5x3 data point, which would make current sets farther from failure than estimated.
- The 0 HOLD/FAIL record over 28 sessions can be read three ways that the current data cannot separate: (a) loads well inside capacity, (b) a slowdown detector that is insensitive, made more so by the 'ambiguous → not a slowdown' tie-break, or (c) appropriate loading during an early adaptation phase. The logged bodyweight being a constant 75 kg, probably a default, also undermines PULL total-load accuracy.
- Evidence that autoregulation beats fixed loading is small and inconsistent: Hickmott 2022 non-significant; Huang 2025 NMA based on few low-quality studies; Mann 2010 not volume-matched and with baseline differences. The benefit of letting load catch up with capacity may be smaller than Graham & Cleather 2021 suggests.
- How much hypertrophy matters for this athlete's long-term squat progress (1.4x to 2.5x BW while losing weight) is not quantified in the proximity literature. Mattocks 2017 shows strength and size dissociating in the short term in untrained people, and this may not hold over multi-year intermediate-to-advanced progression.
- How perceived slowdown accuracy differs between lifts, and whether it improves with REF5-style practice without feedback, is uncertain. Feedback-based improvement (Lazarus; Sindiani) needed a device during the learning phase, which REF5 does not allow.
- How faster load progression or closer proximity would interact with the undiagnosed suprapatellar or quadriceps-tendon pain and the elbow risk ledger (heavy pull-ups, OAP, climbing) is outside the proximity literature reviewed. Any proposal that speeds up loading carries unquantified tendon risk.
- Two cited papers could not be checked against a PubMed abstract because PubMed returned a CAPTCHA. For those, content came from publisher, PMC or repository pages: Pareja-Blanco 2026 (MSSE online first; the author list beyond the first four was not checked) and Robinson 2025 IJSC (the full author list was not checked beyond the first five).
